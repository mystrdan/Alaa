Follow development ↗How Alaa works

Less searching. More doing.

Alaa is being built as a lightweight, offline-first USSD guide for everyday mobile tasks.

01

Search by intent

Start with what you want to do, like checking your balance or buying data, instead of needing to know a code.

02

Know before you dial

Read plain-language descriptions and instructions. Codes stay clearly marked until they have been verified.

03

Useful offline

Browse saved catalogue information without an account. Network access may still be needed to run a USSD session.

Built carefully, not guessed.

Alaa will publish verified codes with source and review information. Unverified suggestions will not become live listings automatically. Never share your mobile money PIN or other private information in a contribution.

Current status: Early development. The catalogue and Android release are not yet production-ready.
